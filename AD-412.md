# AD-412 — Capacity forecasts should stop climbing once they are overdue
Reported by: Dana, on-call lead Priority: Medium

Capacity forecast alerts keep gaining priority after their review date has passed. By the middle of the month there are four or five of them sitting at the top of the digest that nobody has any intention of actioning, and the things that do need attention are below the fold.

Once a capacity forecast is overdue it should hold its current score instead of continuing to climb. Everything else about how it is scored stays as it is.
